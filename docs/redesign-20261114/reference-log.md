# Reference log (Nov 14 2026)

How the references were studied: each site was opened in a real Chrome (Chrome for Testing, GPU WebGL on) at 1440 by 900, left to load, then scrolled with wheel events in small steps so smooth-scroll and scroll-driven scenes followed. Frames were captured at load (1.2 s, 3 s), after settle, and at 5 to 12 scroll stops. For each site the script also recorded loaded fonts, canvas count, the three.js revision, smooth-scroll library, page length and the 3D asset files requested. Contact sheets are in `references/` next to this file; full frames and `info.json` per site are in `~/.t3/portfolio-redesign-20261114/references/<site>/`.

What I could not do: headless capture gives frames, not continuous motion, so easing and timing were read from frame sequences and from each site's shipped libraries, not from watching 60 fps video. Sound was not evaluated. See "Access notes" at the end for sites that resisted capture.

## Decision map

Every row names the reference, what was taken, and how it was changed. Nothing was copied: no code, copy, asset or signature effect.

| Decision | Reference | Taken | Adapted to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Concept: one object, one journey | [igloo.inc](https://www.igloo.inc/) | A single continuous 3D world the scroll travels through, ending where it began | One closed loop of engraved steel band. The camera rides it through seven chapters, and the last chapter pulls out to show it is one figure eight. The loop is the agent loop Arav builds, and the infinity in Infiniflop. Igloo's world is frozen and found; this one is machined and marked. |
| It is about a person | [landonorris.com](https://landonorris.com/) | The name as the hero, first-person lines, chapters that are about the man before the work | "ARAV / JAIN" fills the first screen. Chapters go: who I am, what I build now, rules I build by (his own sentences, each linked to where he wrote it), ventures by year, then work and writing. No portrait: none was approved. |
| Layout: display type behind 3D, reading text in front | [lusion.co](https://lusion.co/) close, where giant words pass behind the astronaut | Real depth between type and object | The canvas sits between two DOM layers. The name, the years and page titles are under the band; reading text is over it. All of it stays real, selectable HTML. |
| Type system | Lando (bold tight grotesk caps for names), Igloo and KPR (small mono HUD) | Two voices: a loud display cut and a quiet technical one | Onest at 700 for the name, years and page titles, Onest 500 for headings, JetBrains Mono 12px uppercase for labels, counts and metadata. Pairing came from taste-skill's deal. No serif, no italics. |
| Colour | Lando (one saturated accent used as fills, grounds that change by chapter), KPR (full-bleed colour grounds) | Commit to one loud colour and let the ground change with the story | Signal yellow `#ffd60a`, black `#0a0a0a`, paper `#f2f2f2`, plus steel that only exists in 3D. Yellow is a ground (hero, close, page heads), not a highlight. Infiniview and the rules run on black. Ventures, work and writing on paper. No gradients anywhere. |
| 3D centrepiece and material | Igloo (physical material, light doing the work), Lusion (studio-lit objects) | Believable material under studio light, not glow | Brushed steel with anisotropic highlights, a roughness map of brushed streaks, laser-style engraving (ruler ticks, counts, words), fully rounded edges. Lit by a procedural studio: softboxes and a floor that takes the page colour, so the underside of the band picks up yellow on yellow chapters and goes dark on black ones. No bloom, no particles, no blur blobs. |
| Engraving on the object | Igloo's labels and readouts on objects | Small technical marks that reward a close look | The marks are on the object itself: a ruler, slot counts, "ARAV JAIN", "AGENTS", "DEV TOOLS", "INFINIFLOP LABS" on one face; "BUILD PROBE BREAK FIX AGAIN" on the other. They slide along the band, so the loop is visibly running. |
| Labels on leader lines | Igloo ("PORTFOLIO_CO_01") | DOM labels pinned to points in the 3D scene | In the Infiniview chapter three tags hang off the probe rings: Review, Probe, Stress-test. Those are Infiniflop's own three verbs, so the labels are content, not decoration. |
| Parallax | Lando (photos at different depths), Lusion | Layers that move at different rates | The two halves of the name part like doors while the camera drops to the band. In the ventures run, the giant years travel slower than the cards and sit behind the band. Pointer movement slides the camera on its own axes. Body copy is never parallaxed. |
| Scroll choreography | Igloo (camera on scroll), David Whyte (3D stays behind long reading) | Scroll as the only timeline, with damping | One rAF loop reads scroll, computes the chapter and progress under the middle of the screen, and the camera follows poses with a critically damped follow. Two pinned chapters only (rules, ventures). The band dissolves before the long lists so reading is undisturbed. |
| Motion curves | Lando and Lusion (Lenis-style glide), Emil Kowalski's tables | Strong ease-out for entrances, strong ease-in-out for on-screen moves | Two tokens only: `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` and `cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)`. Lenis at lerp 0.11. UI feedback 160 to 300ms; the load and the wipe are the only long moves. |
| Loading moment | Skhiladze (loader as an object), Errormade (entrance as a shape) | The loader should be the first frame of the design, not a spinner | A count from 000 to 100 set in the same cut and position as the name, with a hairline. At 100 the count leaves upward through its own baseline as the name rises and the band flies in from depth. Skipped under reduced motion. |
| Page transitions | KPR and Lusion (full-bleed colour changes) | A hard, flat wipe | A black sheet rises, shows the destination in display type, and leaves through the top. 420ms in, 540ms out. Keyboard-activated links skip it. |
| Navigation | Igloo and Jesper (almost nothing, in the corners) | Chrome that stays out of the way | A wordmark and three mono links, no bar, no blur. Ink flips with the ground. It leaves while you read down and returns on the first scroll up. A chapter index and progress hairline sit bottom right in the 3D chapters. |
| Hero | Lando (name and face fill the screen), Tom de Koning (name as the composition) | The name is the layout | Two four-letter lines, left and right aligned, with one steel strand and its underside crossing them. Role and status in the bottom corners. |
| Statement | landing-page-design B11, Lando's big mixed-weight statement | One paragraph read word by word | Words light up in reading order as they cross a line two thirds down the screen. The loop stands to the right. |
| Infiniview chapter | Igloo's ring portal | A ring structure the camera passes | The band runs through fourteen thin yellow rings; one bright ring travels down the line like a scan. Copy is Infiniflop's own sentence, nothing added. |
| Rules chapter | KPR's stacked statement cards, Lando's quotes | One statement at a time at display size | Four of Arav's own sentences, pinned, each with its source link; the loop turns one notch per rule as a small sculpture in the corner. |
| Ventures chapter | Lando's horizontal moments, KPR's giant numerals | A sideways run with huge numbers | Vertical scroll becomes a horizontal run past 2020, 2024, 2026. On phones it is a plain column. |
| Work and writing | Lucas Lima ("things I make on the side" lists), Pangram's strict rows | Honest lists, no screenshots | Numbered rows, name large, one line each, kind and stack in mono. Row hover floods yellow and the cursor says "Open". No project screenshots at all, by design. |
| Close | Igloo (returns to the opening shot) | End on the whole object | The camera pulls out until the entire figure eight floats above the email address. |
| Custom cursor | Jesper (grab cursor), Lando (small cursor labels) | Cursor detail only where it says something | The system cursor stays. A small black label follows it over rows, posts and the email ("Open", "Read", "Write"). |
| Blog shell | David Whyte (calm reading over a quiet world) | The 3D steps back for reading | Blog index gets the yellow head with the band; posts are paper, 720px measure, no canvas and no WebGL download. |
